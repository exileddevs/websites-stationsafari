Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short

Station Safari does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you or your child enter stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Who we are

Station Safari is made by Exiled Developers ("we", "us"). You can contact us at dave@exileddevs.com.

What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • The profile names, colours and icons you choose, and display settings such as sound and animations
  • Which stations have been collected, when, and any star rating or note you add to a station
  • The country, networks and regions you pick, and which networks you have unlocked
  • The parent passcode you set

We never receive this information. We suggest using a first name or nickname for a profile rather than a full name, and not putting personal details in station notes.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in
  • It shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers
  • It has no analytics, tracking or crash reporting tools
  • It does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos
  • It does not send what you enter to any server

Children

Children can use Station Safari with a parent or carer. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect any from children. Purchases and the parent settings are protected by a passcode that the parent sets.

Purchases

Unlocking more networks is done with a one-time tier purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment and we never see your payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase. There are no subscriptions.

Links to other websites

A few screens show a credit for the data a network is built from, and some of these are links. Tapping one opens your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Backups and deleting your data

The app is set not to back up its data to Google or any other service. Your data lives only on your device. Deleting a profile in the app deletes the stations, ratings and notes that belong to it, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log visitors' technical details such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
