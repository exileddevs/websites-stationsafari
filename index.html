Collect every station you visit

Station Safari is a station collecting app. Tap the train stations you have been to and collect them across real train, metro and tram networks around the world.

  • Made for little explorersCelebrate each milestone as your collection grows.
  • Great for grown-up explorers tooCommuters, rail fans and weekend wanderers can tick off every stop and see how far a collection can go.
  • Works offlineEverything is on your device, so it works on a train with no signal.
  • No ads, no subscriptionsFree to try, with one-time purchases to unlock more networks. Nothing recurring.
  • Private by designNothing is collected. Names, ratings and notes stay on your device.

Where can you play?

London, Great Britain, Dublin and Ireland, Sydney and New South Wales, Melbourne and Victoria, Brisbane and South East Queensland, Perth, Adelaide, Toronto, Calgary, Montreal, Auckland and Wellington, with more on the way.

Station Safari is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the transport operators or authorities whose networks it features.
