Help test Station Safari

Thank you for helping! Station Safari is an Android app where you tap the train stations you have visited to collect them, across real networks. It is made for little explorers, and anyone is welcome to play. It is in closed testing on Google Play, and your feedback will help make it better before it opens to everyone.

How to join

You need an Android phone and a Google account. Joining takes three short steps, and you need all of them.

  1. Join the testers group. Open the Station Safari testers group and ask to join, using the Google account that is on your phone.
  2. Become a tester. On your Android phone, signed in with the same Google account, open the testing page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install the app. Follow the link on that page to install Station Safari from Google Play.

It can take a little while after you join before Google Play shows the app. If it says the app is not available, wait a few minutes and try again.

What to check

You do not need to know anything about trains. The most useful things to check are:

  • Is it easy to follow? Could you work out what to do from the first screen without help? Tell us where you got stuck or confused.
  • Does the purchase flow make sense? After the free stations, tap an unlock and go through the steps. See the note on purchases below.
  • Does anything break? Crashes, freezes, things that look wrong or do not respond. If you can, tell us your phone model and Android version.
  • Do the extras work? The themes in Settings (including Calm), setting up more than one profile, and the celebrations when you collect stations and reach milestones.

If you know trains, there is one more thing to look for:

  • Station and line data. Collect stations on a network you know well, including lines that run across more than one network, such as the Sydney to Melbourne XPT or London's Overground. Let us know about a station on the wrong line, a missing stop or a wrong name.

You can read more about how the app works on the gameplay page.

About purchases

Testers do not pay for anything. Before you confirm a purchase, check that the payment method says Test card, always approves.

  • It can take a few hours after you join for the test card to show. If you see a real card, do not confirm. Wait a while and try again.
  • If a real charge does go through by accident, email us and we will refund it.

Send your feedback

Email dave@exileddevs.com, or start a conversation in the testers group. Screenshots help a lot.

Your privacy

The app has no ads, no subscriptions and no tracking, and it does not collect your data. Your collection stays on your device. See the privacy policy for the details.
