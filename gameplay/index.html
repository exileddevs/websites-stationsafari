How Station Safari works

Ride a line, tap the station, watch your collection grow. Here is what you will find in the app.

Collect stations

  1. Pick a network. Choose from real train, metro and tram networks. Each one shows how far through it you are.
  2. Choose a line. Every line lists its stations in order, with a progress bar at the top. Where a line splits, you can switch branch.
  3. Tap the stations you have visited. Each one is ticked off your list. A station shared by several lines or networks only needs collecting once.
  4. Celebrate. Collect a station and the app congratulates you. Finish a line or reach a big milestone and you get a bigger celebration.
  • Pick a network
  • Tick off stations line by line
  • Celebrate each station
  • Finish a whole line

Make it yours

Give each station a star rating from one to five and jot a short note, such as where you were headed or what you spotted. Both stay on your device.

  • Rate stations
  • Add a note

Add the networks you ride

Manage Networks lists every network in the app. Tap Add to bring one in, and the networks you have added sit at the top, ready to play. You can remove a network at any time.

  • Add the networks you ride

Big networks, bite-sized

Some networks, like National Rail across Great Britain, cover a huge area. You can start with the regions nearest your home and add more whenever you like, for example once you have finished your home region.

  • Choose your regions

Profiles for everyone

Add a profile for each person who collects. Every profile keeps its own collection, ticks and theme, and you can switch between them from the home screen.

Choose your theme

Each profile picks a look. There are two presets, and you can mix your own.

Trainspotter

A scenic background, fun animations and themed celebration cards, such as a departures board or a ticket machine.

Calm

A plain white background, gentle fades with no movement, and plain celebration cards. Good for a quieter, simpler look.

Custom

Choose a background and switch animations and themed celebrations on or off to suit you.

  • Calm or Trainspotter

The scenic backgrounds:

  • Highlands
  • Rainforest
  • Old town
  • Savanna

Free to try, three simple tiers

Every network is free to try: you can collect up to four stations on each one before anything needs unlocking. After that, a tier unlocks more networks. Each tier is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to renew.

  • Tier 1Unlock 1 network.
  • Tier 2Unlock up to 3 networks.
  • UnlimitedUnlock as many networks as you like.

Your tier's unlocks are used automatically. A network takes one when you collect its fifth station, and it stays unlocked for good. You can move up to a bigger tier whenever you are ready.

The free trial is shared by every profile on the device, so a station counts once however many profiles collect it. There are no ads and no subscriptions, and purchases are protected by a passcode. See the privacy policy for how little we collect.
